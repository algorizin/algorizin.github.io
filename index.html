Sixty ideas

Hand-picked from nearly two thousand by the demand behind them and by whether one of my companies already reaches the customer. One goes live each week as I write it up; the full research comes once we're talking.

The match

Every founder is missing half. Builders lack distribution; sellers lack a product. I map what you don't have and back you with it. Whatever it takes.

The company

Co-founder, equity. Not an agency, not an accelerator. I form the US company, work through the immigration and visa questions with you, and bring the team, the audience and fifteen years of running companies in these markets.

The match

You bring one thing. I bring the rest.

A company needs someone who builds and someone who sells. Bring either one. I cover what's missing, and everything around it.

You bring

  • You build — real products, shipped fast
  • Or you sell — a pipeline you've run, or an audience that listens

I bring

  • A brand 220,000 students and thousands of placed immigrants already trust
  • My three companies and their networks — HigherStudyAbroad, Algorizin, XenosWealth
  • Engineering or go-to-market, whichever you don't bring — Algorizin's team builds, or opens the first ten conversations
  • Capital, when the numbers work
  • A US company formed for you, with the immigration and visa questions handled
  • Fifteen years of operating experience, the mistakes already made, and weekly working sessions with me on strategy, pricing and hiring
Who

Who I'm looking for

Immigrant founders, solo founders ideally. International students and international founders get preference — I've been one, I've built for hundreds of thousands of them, and I know the constraints. Two profiles, and you only need to be one.

The Builder

Builds

A 10× engineer who ships with AI tools at rocket speed and cares how it feels to use.

You

  • Build end to end — backend, frontend, data, deploy — alone if you have to
  • Fluent with today's AI coding tools; a working product in weeks, not quarters
  • Product-minded: you sweat the user experience, not just the architecture
  • Have shipped something real, even if nobody bought it

What I put in for a Builder

  • Distribution — the customers, communities and channels my companies already reach
  • The first ten conversations, opened for you

The Seller

Sells

Someone who moves people — through a pipeline they've run, or an audience that already listens.

You

  • Can sell anything to anyone, and prove it with a pipeline you've run
  • Or you make content people actually consume, with a distribution you already own
  • Live inside a customer community and speak its language
  • Comfortable charging money from day one

What I put in for a Seller

  • Engineering — Algorizin's team builds the product while you sell
  • The operating playbook from companies that already sell to this audience
Process

How it works

Pick an idea, or bring yours

Any one-pager above, or something you've seen that I haven't.

A thirty-minute call

Builder or seller, what you've shipped or sold, where you are in the world.

I map the gaps

I say plainly what you're missing and what I'd put in.

Ninety days to revenue

Your full commitment for ninety days. Company formed, product or pipeline live, first revenue. Then we decide together how big it gets.

Ready?

Thirty minutes, no deck needed. Bring what you've built or sold and the idea you can't stop thinking about.
