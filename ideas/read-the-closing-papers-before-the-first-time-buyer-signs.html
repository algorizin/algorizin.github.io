Read the closing papers before the first-time buyer signs

An assistant that reads a first-time buyer's closing packet, explains every fee in plain English, and flags the charges that look inflated, duplicated or open to negotiation.

For an immigrant, the first house is the proof that the whole journey worked. It is also the moment they are most exposed. A week before closing, a thick packet arrives full of fees with names like document preparation and administrative charge. Nobody in the family has done this before in this country. They don't know what is normal, what is padding, or what they are allowed to question, so they sign all of it. I have watched people who fought for every dollar give some of it away at the last table because they were afraid to ask.

This product reads the whole packet before the buyer does. It explains each line in plain English, compares the fees with what is typical in that area, and marks anything that looks inflated, duplicated or negotiable. The buyer walks into closing with a short list: these are fine, these you can push back on, and here is how to say it. Agents can hand it to their clients under their own brand, because a buyer who understands the paperwork closes calmer and sends friends.

Why now

Language models can now read a long, messy financial document and explain it line by line, which was not practical until recently. Searches for "calculating closing costs" sit at 40,500/month, +28% over 4 years. Buyers are already looking for this answer; nobody is handing it to them at the right moment.

Who it's for

First-time home buyers, starting with immigrant families buying their first house in the US, then the real estate agents who want to give every client the same protection.

What gets built

  • A document upload that reads closing disclosures and turns them into a plain-English breakdown with red flags highlighted.
  • A regional fee reference built from public records, so every charge is judged against what is normal nearby.
  • A white-label version agents can give their buyers under their own name.
  • A free closing checklist, a guided plan at $9/month, $50–$100 per closing for the pro suite, and agent subscriptions at $500–$2,000/month.
