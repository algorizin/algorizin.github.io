Turn a markdown proposal into a branded client PDF

A tool that takes the proposal a technical freelancer wrote in markdown and exports it as a branded, designer-quality PDF in one click, with their logo, fonts and colors saved once.

Engineers write in plain text — notes, specs, proposals — because it is fast and it is how they think. Then the proposal has to go to a client, and suddenly it needs a logo, the right fonts and a layout that looks like a real business sent it. So the text gets copied into a design tool, the formatting breaks, and an hour goes to making it look right. For an immigrant engineer going independent and trying to look established in front of a US client, that polish matters more than it should, and it costs more time than the writing did.

The product sits at the moment of export. A freelancer drops in the markdown, picks a template that already carries their brand, previews it, and gets a PDF that looks like a designer made it. Brand settings are saved once and applied to every document after that. Templates cover the documents freelancers actually send — proposals, scopes of work, project briefs. Nothing about the writing changes, and nobody has to leave the editor they like. The finished document simply stops costing an hour every time a client is waiting for it.

Why now

More writing now starts in markdown than ever, because note-taking apps and AI tools both produce it by default, and searches for "markdown to pdf" sit at 201,000/month, +75% over 4 years. The converters that exist produce plain documents. The proposal tools that produce branded ones make you abandon your editor.

Who it's for

Technical freelancers and consultants who send proposals every week first, then small agencies that want every document out the door to look the same.

What gets built

  • An upload-and-export flow that renders markdown into a branded PDF in seconds, with a preview before export.
  • Brand settings — logo, colors, typography — saved once per account and applied to every document.
  • A set of professional templates for proposals, scopes of work and project briefs, with a marketplace for more later.
  • A free quick-start guide, a $10/month starter plan, a $30/month pro plan, and extra templates at $5–$25 each.
