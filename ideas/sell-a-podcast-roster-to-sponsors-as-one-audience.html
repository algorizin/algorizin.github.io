Sell a podcast roster to sponsors as one audience

A tool for small podcast agencies that combines listener data across every show they represent and turns it into a bundled media kit a sponsor can buy in one go.

Small podcast agencies have a packaging problem, not a reach problem. They represent a handful of shows and pitch them to sponsors one at a time, as if a parenting show, a career show and a cooking show had nothing to do with each other. On its own, each show looks too small for a brand to bother with. Together, they might be exactly the audience a home-goods or productivity brand is hunting for. As someone who spends marketing money across several companies, I would rather buy one well-described audience than chase a long list of small ones.

This product does the packaging. It pulls listener data from the hosting platforms and the big listening apps, finds where audiences overlap across an agency's shows, and proposes bundles that make sense for a specific kind of sponsor. Then it writes the media kit and the pitch deck — combined reach, shared traits, why this brand fits — filled in from the agency's own numbers. The agency walks into a sponsor meeting with one offer instead of a stack of one-sheets, and gets paid like a network instead of a collection of small shows.

Why now

Brands now put real budget into podcasts, but the buying tools are built for big networks and single shows, not the small agency in between. Searches for "podcast sponsorship" are at 5,400/month, up 29% over 4 years, even as broader podcast ad searches cool. The listener data needed to build bundles already sits in the hosting platforms, so this is a software problem, not a sales one.

Who it's for

Small agencies that represent a handful of independent podcasts, then the independent networks and ad sales reps who sell on shows' behalf.

What gets built

  • Connectors to podcast hosting platforms and listening apps that pull listener demographics into one portfolio view.
  • An overlap engine that proposes show bundles matched to a sponsor category, with the combined reach worked out.
  • A media kit and pitch deck generator that fills itself from the agency's own data.
  • A free case study on bundled sales first, then $49/month for the first 3 months, $299/month for the standard agency package, and a $99/month analytics add-on.
