Write the launch emails in the teacher's own voice

An email agent for solo course creators that learns how the teacher writes, then drafts and schedules a full launch sequence timed to the next cohort's dates.

Online teachers sell on trust. Students buy a course because they believe the person teaching it, and email is where that belief is built or lost. Most solo educators know this and still send too little, because writing a launch sequence is a week of work they would rather spend on the material. The templates they can buy sound like a marketing company, and an agency costs more than a teacher working alone can carry. HigherStudyAbroad lives on enrollment, and I know how much revenue hides in the gap between a good course and an email nobody wrote.

This agent reads the course outline, the cohort calendar and a handful of emails the teacher has already sent. It writes the whole launch in that voice — the announcement, the reminders, the case for the course, and the cart-close in a few different tones: urgent, results-led, and personal. The teacher reads the drafts, changes a line, approves, and the sequence goes out on schedule while they sleep. The measure of success is simple: how little the teacher has to edit before pressing approve, and how many seats fill when the cart closes.

Why now

Language models can now hold a person's voice across a long sequence well enough that the edits stay light, which was not true until recently. Searches for "email marketing emails" are at 550,000/month, +147% over 4 years, and solo educators are a large slice of that demand with nobody writing for them.

Who it's for

Solo educators running cohort-based courses with fixed launch dates come first, then coaches and creators selling evergreen programs.

What gets built

  • An intake that reads the curriculum, the cohort dates and past emails to learn the teacher's voice and cadence.
  • A launch sequence generator that drafts every email and several cart-close variants, each timed to the cohort calendar.
  • An approve-and-schedule screen that connects to the teacher's email tool and sends on their behalf.
  • A free marketing webinar to start, a $49 one-time starter kit, then $99/month for the core product and $149/month for advanced features.
